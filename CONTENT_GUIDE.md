# Content guide

The initial dataset is intentionally a starter set. Before distributing the app:
1. Compare chapter list and sequence against the current academic-year KSEAB/DSERT textbook.
2. Have a subject teacher verify worked examples, answer choices and all Kannada translations.
3. Add chapter-wise exercise sets and worked solutions from an authorised source.
4. Keep a `source`, `academic_year`, `medium`, and `verified_by` field for each official paper.
5. Never present the demo questions as official past-board questions.
